# CleanQuote Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 14, 2026**

CleanQuote ("the App") is provided by **SPCOMPUCARE**. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use CleanQuote.

## Information Used by CleanQuote

CleanQuote helps cleaning professionals create estimates, calculate pricing, prepare customer quotes, save jobs, and compare estimated work with actual job results.

Users may choose to enter information such as:

- Customer names
- Customer phone numbers
- Customer email addresses
- Job notes
- Property and cleaning-job details
- Pricing and quote information
- Estimated and actual job times
- App settings and preferences

## Local Data Storage

CleanQuote stores job, quote, customer, and settings information locally on the user's device.

CleanQuote does not require a CleanQuote account and does not operate a CleanQuote cloud service for storing this information.

Information entered into the App is used to provide its functionality, including creating estimates, calculating prices, preparing quotes, tracking jobs, and comparing estimated and actual results.

## Data Sharing

CleanQuote does not sell user or customer information.

CleanQuote does not intentionally transmit customer, quote, or job information entered into the App to the developer or share this information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Data Security

CleanQuote relies on security protections provided by the user's device and operating system for locally stored application data.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on their devices.

No method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored by CleanQuote remains locally on the user's device unless it is removed through available App functionality, the App's storage is cleared, or the App is uninstalled.

Because CleanQuote does not require an online account or store this information on CleanQuote-operated servers, the developer generally does not have access to or the ability to remotely delete information stored locally on a user's device.

## Third-Party Services

CleanQuote may rely on standard platform services and software libraries required to distribute and operate the App.

Any data handling performed by third-party services is subject to the applicable third party's terms and privacy practices.

## Children's Privacy

CleanQuote is intended as a business productivity tool for cleaning professionals and is not designed or directed toward children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if CleanQuote's features or data-handling practices change. Any updated policy will be published with a revised effective date.
