# Cleanometry Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: October 3, 2026**

Cleanometry ("the App") is provided by **SPCOMPUCARE**.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Cleanometry, including information stored locally on your device and information that may be processed by third-party services used for advertising and purchases.

## Information Used by Cleanometry

Cleanometry is a business productivity application designed to help cleaning professionals and small cleaning businesses create estimates, calculate pricing, prepare customer quotes, save jobs, and compare estimated work with actual job results.

Users may choose to enter information such as:

- Customer names
- Customer phone numbers
- Customer email addresses
- Job notes
- Property and cleaning-job details
- Pricing and quote information
- Estimated and actual job times
- App settings and preferences

## Local Data Storage

Customer, job, quote, pricing, and settings information entered into Cleanometry is stored locally on the user's device.

Cleanometry does not require users to create a Cleanometry account and does not operate a Cleanometry cloud service for storing this information.

Information entered into the App is used to provide Cleanometry's functionality, including creating estimates, calculating prices, preparing quotes, tracking jobs, generating invoices, and comparing estimated and actual job results.

## Advertising

Cleanometry may display advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**.

Google and its advertising partners may process certain information in connection with providing, measuring, securing, and personalizing advertisements. Depending on the user's device, location, privacy choices, and applicable law, this information may include device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, interactions with advertisements, diagnostic information, and other technical information.

Cleanometry does not intentionally provide Google AdMob with customer names, customer contact information, job notes, quote contents, invoice contents, property details, or other customer or job information that users enter into Cleanometry.

Google's use of information in connection with its advertising services is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google handles information:

https://business.safety.google/privacy/

## Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Cleanometry uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising-related consent and privacy choices where required.

For users in regions where consent or privacy choices are required, including where applicable the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland, Cleanometry may display a Google consent message before requesting advertisements.

The choices available may depend on the user's location and applicable privacy requirements.

Where required, users can review or change applicable advertising privacy choices using the **Privacy options** control available in Cleanometry's Settings.

Cleanometry checks whether advertising may be requested before loading advertisements.

## Remove Ads Purchase

Cleanometry may offer an optional one-time **Remove Ads** purchase.

Purchases are processed through **Google Play** using Google Play Billing. Cleanometry does not directly collect or process users' payment card or bank account information.

Google Play may process information necessary to complete, verify, restore, and manage purchases in accordance with Google's terms and privacy practices.

Cleanometry stores the user's Remove Ads entitlement locally so that the App can remember that advertising has been removed.

Users who have purchased Remove Ads may also be able to restore their purchase through the App.

## Data Sharing

Cleanometry does not sell customer, job, quote, or invoice information entered into the App.

Cleanometry does not intentionally transmit customer, quote, job, or invoice content entered into the App to the developer or provide that content to Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

Certain technical, device, advertising, consent, and purchase-related information may be processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob, Google's User Messaging Platform, Google Play, and Google Play Billing as necessary to provide their respective services.

Third-party processing is governed by the applicable provider's terms, privacy policies, consent choices, and applicable law.

## Data Security

Cleanometry relies on security protections provided by the user's device and operating system for locally stored application data.

Users are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on their devices.

No method of electronic storage, transmission, or processing can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Customer, job, quote, and settings information stored by Cleanometry remains locally on the user's device unless it is removed through available App functionality, the App's storage is cleared, or the App is uninstalled.

Because Cleanometry does not require an online Cleanometry account and does not store this information on Cleanometry-operated servers, the developer generally does not have access to or the ability to remotely delete information stored locally on a user's device.

Information processed independently by third-party services may be retained according to those providers' respective policies and legal obligations.
